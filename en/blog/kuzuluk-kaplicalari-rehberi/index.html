Kuzuluk Hot Springs Guide: A Thermal Break from Sapanca

26 August 2026Updated: 16 November 20264 min readSapanca

The Kuzuluk hot springs are in Kuzuluk, in the Akyazı district of Sakarya, about 47 km (35–45 minutes) by road from Kırkpınar. According to an academic geology study, the spring's surface temperature is about 51 °C and its flow is 42 litres per second; this hot water is fed to the pools and cabins of the thermal facilities in the area. Staying in Sapanca and setting aside an afternoon for Kuzuluk is the most practical way to balance a nature trip with a relaxing break, especially on autumn and winter weekends.

Summary
  • Location: Kuzuluk, Akyazı / Sakarya — ~47 km from Kırkpınar, 35–45 min.
  • Water: Spring surface temperature ~51 °C (academic measurement); pool temperature varies by facility.
  • Best season: Autumn and winter; the best plan B for rainy days.
  • Health: People with chronic conditions and pregnant women should consult a doctor first.

Where are the Kuzuluk hot springs and how do you get there from Sapanca?

Kuzuluk is a settlement east of Adapazarı, in the Akyazı district. From Kırkpınar you reach it by heading east on the Anatolian Motorway (TEM) and turning off towards Akyazı; according to map routing the road is about 47 km and around 35 minutes without traffic. Since the area can be busy on weekend afternoons and during holidays, allowing 45 minutes is more realistic.

How hot is the water at Kuzuluk?

In a geology study of Kuzuluk's thermal water, the temperature of the spring at the surface is given as 51 °C and its flow as 42 litres per second. The pool water in the facilities is brought to a usable temperature by cooling and mixing the spring water; we recommend asking the facility about the pool temperature before you go.

What facilities are there in Kuzuluk?

In addition to businesses offering day-use pools and cabins, Kuzuluk also has thermal hotels with accommodation; for example Kuzuluk Termal, owned by the İhlas group, is one of the well-known facilities in the area. Day-entry conditions, prices and family/private cabin options vary from one business to another, so we do not give prices here; calling by phone is the most reliable way to find out.

What are thermal waters good for?

It is generally known that hot water reduces muscle tension and relaxes you; however, for claims that thermal water "is good for" a particular illness, a doctor's opinion is essential. In cases such as heart and circulatory conditions, high blood pressure, pregnancy or open wounds, consult your doctor before entering a thermal pool. For healthy adults too the general rule is the same: do not stay in hot water for a long time at a stretch, rest in between and drink plenty of water.
