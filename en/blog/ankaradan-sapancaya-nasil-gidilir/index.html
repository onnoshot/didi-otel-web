How to Get from Ankara to Sapanca: High-Speed Train, Car and Bus Guide

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

The fastest way to get from Ankara to Sapanca is the high-speed train (YHT): trains leaving Ankara Station reach Arifiye in Sakarya in about 2 hours 36–39 minutes. The YHT does not stop at Sapanca; from Arifiye it is roughly 13 km by road to Kırkpınar (about 15 minutes by taxi). By private car, Ankara to Sapanca is about 320 km and takes around 3.5 hours without traffic. According to the October 2026 timetable, there are 6 YHT services a day from Ankara to Arifiye.

Summary
  • YHT: Ankara → Arifiye ~2 h 37 min, 6 services a day; Arifiye → Kırkpınar ~13 km.
  • Car: ~320 km, ~3.5 hours; leave the motorway at the Sapanca exit, and the hotel is ~500 m from the toll gates.
  • Night train: the Ankara Express leaves at 22:00 and arrives in Arifiye at 04:24.
  • Times can change; confirm them on TCDD e-bilet.

If you are leaving on a Friday evening

If you set off after work, the 17:25 and 18:30 services are the best options: you arrive in Arifiye at 20:02 or 21:07 and reach Kırkpınar by taxi in about 15 minutes. Just let us know about a late arrival in advance and we will prepare your check-in accordingly.

How do you get from Arifiye station to Sapanca?

Arifiye station is on the Adapazarı–Sapanca road. According to OpenStreetMap routing, the distance between the station and Kırkpınar is about 13 km, which takes 15 minutes without traffic. Your options are:

  • Taxi: The most practical option, straight from the station forecourt to the hotel.
  • Ada Ekspresi: The Gebze–Adapazarı regional train stops at both Arifiye and Sapanca. However, with only 6 services a day, matching it to your YHT arrival is a matter of luck, and from Sapanca station it is still about 5 km to Kırkpınar.
  • Car rental: Makes sense if you want to explore Kartepe, Maşukiye and Kuzuluk over the weekend.

By car from Ankara to Sapanca

It is about 320 km by car from Ankara to Sapanca, which takes around 3.5 hours on the motorway without traffic. Heading towards Istanbul, you pass Düzce and Sakarya and leave the motorway at the Sapanca exit. DİDİ Otel Sapanca is in Kırkpınar, about 500 metres from the motorway's Sapanca toll gates, and free parking is available for our guests.

  • In winter there can be snow and fog on the Bolu Mountain section; check the General Directorate of Highways' road-condition announcements before you leave.
  • The motorway is busy on the first day of long weekends; early morning is easier.
  • The service areas at Bolu and Düzce are good places for a break along the way.

By bus from Ankara to Sapanca

Many bus companies running from Ankara AŞTİ to Istanbul and Sakarya pass through the Sapanca area. Some drop you at their own office in Sapanca, some at a point on the motorway, and some only at the Adapazarı bus station. When you buy your ticket, be sure to ask "Where do I get off in Sapanca?"; from wherever you get off, you can reach Kırkpınar by taxi in a short time.

For those coming from Eskişehir

Eskişehir is on the same YHT line. According to the timetable compilation, there are 12 services a day between Arifiye and Eskişehir, and the shortest journey takes about 1 hour 8 minutes, which is why Sapanca is so close for a weekend break from Eskişehir.

Which option suits whom?

The right way to get from Ankara to Sapanca depends on how many of you are travelling and what you want to do once you are there.

  • Solo travellers or couples, short weekend: The YHT makes the most sense. You rest on the way and are at the hotel 15 minutes after Arifiye by taxi. Nearby stops such as the cable car, the lake shore and Maşukiye are easy to reach by taxi.
  • Family of four: As the number of tickets grows, coming in your own car wins on both cost and flexibility; with children, getting to scattered spots such as Ormanya, Kartepe and Kuzuluk is also easier by car.
  • Winter weekend: Because of the chance of snow and fog on the Bolu Mountain section, the YHT is the safe choice if the weather is bad; if you want to go up to Kartepe, you can rent a car in Sapanca for the day.
  • Religious holidays and long weekends: Expect heavy traffic on the motorway, and YHT tickets sell out quickly. Buying your ticket as soon as it goes on sale is the best strategy.

How should you plan the return journey?

There are two common mistakes on the way back on Sunday: leaving the room early without waiting for check-out time (12:00) and wasting the day, or trying to catch the last train and arriving late at Arifiye. After check-out you can leave your luggage with us and take one last walk along the lake shore, have lunch in Maşukiye and head back on an afternoon YHT. Always check the Arifiye–Ankara return times on the TCDD e-bilet system and arrive at the station at least 20–30 minutes early; there can be traffic on the Adapazarı–Sapanca road on weekend afternoons.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you get from Ankara to Sapanca by train?

Take the YHT from Ankara Station to Arifiye (about 2 hours 36–39 minutes). The YHT does not stop at Sapanca; from Arifiye it is about 13 km by road to Kırkpınar, 15 minutes by taxi.

How many YHT services a day run from Ankara to Arifiye?

According to the October 2026 timetable, there are 6 YHT services a day from Ankara Station to Arifiye: 06:25, 08:40, 10:50, 12:35, 17:25 and 18:30. There is also the Ankara Express, which leaves at 22:00.

How many km is it from Ankara to Sapanca?

It is about 320 km by road; on the motorway without traffic it takes about 3.5 hours.

Does the YHT stop at Sapanca?

No. The YHT stop in Sakarya is Arifiye. Only the Ada Ekspresi regional train, running between Gebze and Adapazarı, stops at Sapanca station.

How do you get from Arifiye station to Sapanca?

The most practical way is a taxi; Arifiye to Kırkpınar is about 13 km and 15 minutes. The Ada Ekspresi also runs from Arifiye to Sapanca, but with 6 services a day the times have to line up.

How long does it take to get from Eskişehir to Sapanca?

By YHT, Eskişehir–Arifiye takes about 1 hour 8 minutes at the shortest; add about 15 minutes by taxi from Arifiye to Kırkpınar.
