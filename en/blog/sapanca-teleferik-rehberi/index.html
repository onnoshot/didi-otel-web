Sapanca Cable Car Guide: Ticket Prices, Hours and the Line from Kırkpınar

13 August 2026Updated: 16 November 20265 min readSapanca

Sapanca Teleferik (the Sapanca cable car) is a 1,501-metre line that runs from the lower station in Kırkpınar up to the upper station on the Mahmudiye side, giving you a bird's-eye view of the lake. According to the operator's official price list (checked on 16 November 2026), the full return ticket is 450 TL and the student ticket is 300 TL; the facility is open every day from 11:00 to 19:00. Tickets are sold only at the ticket office, with no online sales. The upper station has a zipline, ATV safaris and walking trails.

Summary
  • Line: 1,501 m long, 319 m difference in height, 36 cabins (8 people each).
  • Price: Full 450 TL, student 300 TL (return). One-way tickets are also sold.
  • Hours: Every day 11:00–19:00. Phone: 444 6 345.
  • Address: Kırkpınar Hasanpaşa, Şehit Cevdet Koç Caddesi No:78, Sapanca.

Where does the Sapanca cable car go?

The Sapanca Teleferik starts in Kırkpınar on the southern shore of the lake and climbs towards Mahmudiye at the foot of the Samanlı Mountains. According to the reference page of Leitner, the company that built the line, the system entered service in 2023; the line is 1,501 metres long, with a height difference of 319 metres between the two stations. 36 cabins run on 9 towers, each cabin holds 8 people, and the system's capacity is 1,513 passengers per hour.

The cabins travel at 5 metres per second, so the 1,501 metres take about 5 minutes. With boarding, alighting and the slowdowns at the stations, a one-way trip takes a few minutes longer. For the lake view you leave behind as you climb, sit on the lake-facing side of the cabin.

Why does the price history matter?

The cable car ticket price has been updated several times since the opening in 2023, and pages showing old prices still circulate on the internet. For that reason we suggest relying only on the price list on the operator's own page, and calling ahead if you are travelling with a large group.

What are the cable car's opening hours?

According to the official website, the facility operates every day from 11:00 to 19:00. Cable cars can be stopped in strong wind for safety reasons; especially in winter and on days with a storm warning, calling 444 6 345 before you set out will save you a wasted trip. On weekend afternoons a queue can form at the ticket office; going close to opening time is usually easier.

What is at the upper station?

The operator's website lists the facilities of the upper station area as follows: a zipline, ATV safaris, trekking trails, bungalow accommodation and a café-restaurant with a lake view. Zipline and ATV fees are not included in the cable car ticket. We cover the details of the adventure activities in our Sapanca zipline and ATV guide. For the village atmosphere and markets on the Mahmudiye side, see our article on Mahmudiye and its hidden corners.

The forest is lush green and the lake sparkles. To escape the midday heat, ride up at the 11:00 opening and walk at the top. Because sunset is around 20:30 in June, you cannot watch it from the cabin before closing time in summer; the light is still at its softest in the early evening.

From late October the forests turn yellow and red, and this is the best time to glide above the colourful slopes. In November sunset moves back to around 17:40, so you can watch the sun go down from above before closing time. More in our autumn guide.

On snowy days the peaks of the Samanlı Mountains and the misty lake offer a different view. Services can be stopped because of wind, so be sure to call ahead; bring a thick coat.

How do you get to the cable car from DİDİ Otel?

The lower station of the cable car is in Kırkpınar Hasanpaşa, where DİDİ Otel Sapanca is also located; it is a short distance from the hotel, a few minutes by car. After breakfast you can catch the 11:00 opening and spend time at the upper station until noon, then spend the afternoon by the lake or, in season, by the pool. For the evening, Mare Gastro in the garden welcomes you without you having to go out. You can get the full directions from our reception.

Visiting tips

  • Tickets are sold only at the ticket office; bring a card or cash.
  • If you are a student, don't forget your student ID validated for that year.
  • At the upper station the air is cooler and windier than below; bring a light jacket.
  • If you are going up with a bicycle, ask at the ticket office about the extra fee and the conditions for taking it in the cabin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the Sapanca cable car depart from?

The lower station is in Kırkpınar Hasanpaşa, at Şehit Cevdet Koç Caddesi No:78; the line climbs to the upper station on the Mahmudiye side. DİDİ Otel Sapanca is also in Kırkpınar.

How much is a Sapanca cable car ticket?

According to the operator's official price list (16 November 2026), a full return ticket is 450 TL and a student ticket is 300 TL. War veterans, veterans' spouses and first-degree relatives of martyrs travel free.

What are the Sapanca cable car's opening hours?

According to the official website it operates every day from 11:00 to 19:00. Services can be stopped in strong wind; you can call 444 6 345 before you go.

Can I buy cable car tickets online?

No. According to the official price list there are no online sales; tickets are sold at the ticket office at the lower station and are valid throughout the day they are bought.

How many minutes does the cable car ride take?

The line is 1,501 metres and the cabins travel at 5 metres per second; the ride itself takes about 5 minutes, and with boarding and alighting a one-way trip takes a few minutes longer.

What is there at the upper station?

According to the operator's website, the upper station has a zipline, ATV safaris, trekking trails, bungalows and a café-restaurant with a lake view. These activities are not included in the cable car ticket.
