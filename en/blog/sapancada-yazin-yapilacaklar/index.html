Things to Do in Sapanca in Summer: Pool, SUP, Highlands and Sunsets

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

The top things to do in Sapanca in summer are cooling off in the pool, paddling a SUP on the lake early in the morning, escaping the midday heat to the stream side in Maşukiye or the cool highlands of Kartepe, and watching the sun set around 20:30 from the lake shore. According to Adapazarı's climate normals, the average daytime high in July and August is around 30 °C and there are on average 6 rainy days a month; in other words, summer in Sapanca means long, sunny days. Below you will find a summer programme that makes smart use of the hot hours.

Summary
  • Morning: SUP or cycling (windless hours); the cable car opens at 11:00.
  • Midday: The pool, the stream side in Maşukiye or the cool highlands of Kartepe.
  • Evening: Sunset is ~20:30 in June–July and ~20:00 in August.
  • Safety: Wear a life jacket on the lake; heed sudden-wind warnings.

Because the lake is a drinking-water source, the safest and most enjoyable way to cool off in Sapanca in summer is the pool. SUP on the lake: calm morning water

On summer mornings the lake is usually at its calmest, which makes it ideal for SUP. According to a July 2026 DHA news report, an association that has been popularising SUP on the lake is organising free introductory events. The rules are simple: wear a life jacket, do not stray far from the shore and check the weather forecast. The rescue by AFAD of 35 people who had gone out on the lake in a sudden storm on the evening of 22 July 2026 shows how quickly summer storms can arrive. More details are in our Sapanca Lake activities post.

Escaping the midday heat: Maşukiye and Kartepe

There are two good escapes during the hottest hours. In Maşukiye, you can eat trout in the shade at the stream-side restaurants, or you can drive 25–30 minutes up into the Samanlı Mountains and walk in the cool air of Kartepe and Kuzuyayla. As the altitude rises, the temperature drops noticeably; don't forget to bring a light layer.

Summer with children: Ormanya and adventure

Ormanya, which stays open longer in summer, is a favourite with children thanks to its Hobbit houses and animal-viewing areas. Going early in the morning and spending midday at the pool protects against heatstroke. For a more active day, we have described the zipline and ATV options at the cable car's upper station in our adventure guide. More suggestions for families are in our family holiday in Sapanca post.

How do you avoid the summer weekend crowds?

Summer weekends are Sapanca's busiest period. The most effective way to avoid the crowds is to shift your timings: go to popular spots before everyone else or after everyone else. Riding the cable car at the 11:00 opening, eating lunch in Maşukiye before 12:00 and heading down to the lake shore an hour before sunset let you see the same places far more comfortably. Saturday afternoon and Sunday midday are the busiest slots; spending those hours by the pool or in the garden is the smart move. If you can, arriving on Thursday rather than Friday and heading back early on Sunday morning also noticeably shortens your time on the road.

Summer evenings: timings and small notes

Summer evenings are long; the sun sets around 20:30 in June and July and it stays light until 21:00. Mosquitoes can be around the lake and streams in the late afternoon, so bring repellent. Mare Gastro in the hotel garden is open until 00:00 (last order 23:00), so it is easy for adults to plan a long dinner after sunset. There is a rule families with children should know: children are not admitted to Mare Gastro after 15:00.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do in Sapanca in summer?

Cooling off in the pool, SUP on the lake in the mornings, lunch by the stream in Maşukiye, walks in the cool highlands of Kartepe and Kuzuyayla, the cable car, Ormanya and sunsets on the lake shore are the most popular summer activities.

What is the temperature in Sapanca in summer?

According to the 1991–2020 Adapazarı normals, the average high in July and August is around 30 °C and the average night-time low is around 19 °C.

Can you swim in Lake Sapanca?

The lake is a drinking-water source and there are safety risks; ask the local authority for the current rule. We recommend the pool for cooling off, and SUP with a life jacket to enjoy the lake.

What time is sunset in Sapanca in summer?

The sun sets at around 20:30 in mid-June and mid-July and at around 20:00 in mid-August.

Is there a hotel with a pool in Sapanca?

Yes. The outdoor pool at DİDİ Otel Sapanca is Sapanca's only chlorine-free pool; it is open in the summer season, together with the palm-lined garden and cabanas.

Where can you go with children in Sapanca in summer?

Ormanya, the cable car, a picnic and cycling at Uzunkum, the stream side in Maşukiye and the hotel pool are the best options for families with children.
