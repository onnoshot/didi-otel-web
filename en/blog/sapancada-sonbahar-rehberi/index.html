Autumn in Sapanca: An October–November Travel Guide and Fall Colour Route

16 November 20267 min readSapanca

Autumn in Sapanca is a calm season: the summer crowds are gone and the forests turn yellow and red. According to the 1991–2020 climate normals for Adapazarı, November has an average daytime high of 17 °C and an average night-time low of 8 °C, with around 12 rainy days in the month. The best window for colour is late October to mid-November; the loveliest routes are the Maşukiye valley, the forest road up to Kartepe, Ormanya and the lake views from the cable car. The sun sets at around 17:40 in November, so start the day early and finish it at a warm table or with a thermal break.

Summary
  • When: For colour, late October to mid-November; September still feels like summer.
  • Weather: November averages 8–17 °C with ~12 rainy days; pack a waterproof jacket.
  • Route: Maşukiye, the Kartepe road and Kuzuyayla, Ormanya, the cable car, the Kırkpınar shore.
  • Evening: Sunset ~17:40; a thermal soak in Kuzuluk or a long dinner at the hotel.

What is the weather like in Sapanca in autumn?

The three autumn months are quite different from one another. September still feels like a continuation of summer; in October it cools down and the rain begins; November is the month of cool, damp, short days. The table below is compiled from the 1991–2020 Adapazarı normals (source: Turkish State Meteorological Service); Sapanca lies about 15 km from Adapazarı and has a similar climate.

The practical conclusion: in November there is a chance of rain every second day or so, but the showers usually do not last all day. Mornings are misty, midday is mild and evenings are cool. It is therefore wise to keep your plans flexible and have a rainy-day plan B; for ideas, see our article on what to do in Sapanca in the rain.

When do the autumn colours start?

In the mixed forests around Sapanca (beech, oak, chestnut, hornbeam), the colour change usually begins in the second half of October and reaches its most vivid tones in the first half of November. The exact date changes with the weather each year: in a warm October the colours are late, while an early cold snap speeds things up. The higher areas (Kartepe, Kuzuyayla) colour before the lakeshore, so in mid-November you may still see green by the water while the leaves are already falling on the mountain road.

Maşukiye: the misty stream

The most photogenic autumn mornings happen in Maşukiye: mist over the stream, yellowing trees and sunshine breaking through towards noon. After rain the waterfalls swell too. You can have trout for lunch by the stream and then continue towards Kartepe.

The Kartepe road and Kuzuyayla

The road climbing from Maşukiye to Kartepe is one of the most colourful routes of the season. For a short walk in Kuzuyayla Nature Park, bring a warm layer and waterproof shoes; it is noticeably cooler up high than below. Towards the end of November the first snow can appear at the summit; for the ski season, see our Kartepe ski guide.

Ormanya: a forest walk for families

Ormanya, in Kartepe's Uzuntarla neighbourhood, offers a cool, peaceful walk in autumn. The hobbit houses and animal-watching areas are a hit with children. Closing time moves earlier with the switch to winter hours, so it is a good idea to go before noon.

Over the colours by cable car

The Sapanca Teleferik cable car runs every day from 11:00 to 19:00. Because the sun sets at around 17:40 in November, you can watch the sunset from above before the cable car closes; that opportunity does not exist in summer. Keep in mind that services may stop on windy days.

What to eat in Sapanca in autumn

Autumn is the season of Sakarya's geographically registered speciality, Adapazarı kabak tatlısı (pumpkin dessert); as the pumpkin harvest comes in, the confectioners' windows fill up. A hot ıslama köfte (soaked meatballs in a tomato-butter sauce over bread) in cool weather, or trout by the stream in Maşukiye, also suit the season well. Details are in our guide to what to eat in Sapanca.

The best times for autumn photography

Autumn light makes this the most generous time of year to photograph Sapanca. The sun rises at around 07:10 in mid-October and 07:46 in mid-November; at these hours mist often forms over the lake, and from the Kırkpınar shore the far bank slowly emerges from the fog. In the afternoon, as the sun drops, the colourful slopes are lit with warm light. Sunset is at around 18:20 in October and 17:40 in November; golden hour starts roughly 45 minutes earlier. The days after rain, with light slipping through the clouds and streams in full flow, give the most dramatic shots. You can find where to shoot in our guide to photo and sunset spots.

A midweek autumn getaway

If you want to see Sapanca at its calmest in autumn, one or two midweek nights are unbeatable: the riverside venues of Maşukiye, the cable car and Ormanya are much quieter than at weekends, and there is no Friday-evening rush on the motorway. If you can work remotely, you could start the morning with a walk by the lake, work from your room in the afternoon and head out for sunset in the early evening, a short but full break. What is the weather like in Sapanca in November?

According to the 1991–2020 Adapazarı normals, November has an average high of 17.2 °C and an average low of 8.0 °C, with about 12 rainy days in the month.

Where should I go in Sapanca in autumn?

The Maşukiye valley, the Kartepe road and Kuzuyayla, Ormanya, Sapanca Teleferik, the Kırkpınar shore and Poyrazlar Lake stand out, with Kuzuluk for a thermal break.
