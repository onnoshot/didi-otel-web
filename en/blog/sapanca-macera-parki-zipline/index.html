A Guide to Zipline, ATV and Adventure Activities in Sapanca

26 August 2026Updated: 16 November 20265 min readSapanca

The first address for anyone who wants to zipline in Sapanca is the upper station of the Sapanca Teleferik (cable car): according to the operator's official website, this area reached by cable car offers a zipline, ATV safari and trekking trails together. Beyond that, there are activities such as rope courses and giant swings in the forest adventure parks towards Maşukiye and Kartepe, and SUP (stand-up paddleboarding), which has spread rapidly on the lake in recent years. Below we have gathered where each activity is, who it suits and what to watch out for before you go.

Summary
  • Zipline + ATV: Sapanca Teleferik upper station (lower station in Kırkpınar, open every day 11:00–19:00).
  • Rope courses, giant swing: forest adventure parks on the Maşukiye–Kartepe route.
  • Water: SUP on Lake Sapanca; a life jacket is a must, and do not head out onto the lake in windy weather.
  • Prices and age and weight limits vary by operator; confirm by phone before you go.

Where can you zipline in Sapanca?

The most easily reached zipline is in the upper station area reached by the Sapanca Teleferik. The operator's official website lists a zipline, ATV safari, trekking, bungalows and a café-restaurant with lake views as facilities of the same area; the address is given as the lower station in Kırkpınar Hasanpaşa (Şehit Cevdet Koç Caddesi No:78). So you first ride up the 1,501-metre line by cable car, then choose your activities at the top.

The length, price and age and weight limits of the zipline are not published on the official page; the cable car ticket is sold separately. Before you go, we recommend getting current information from the operator's line on 444 6 345. Cable car tickets are sold only at the ticket office and are valid throughout the day they are bought.

Adventure parks in Maşukiye and Kartepe

On the road to Maşukiye, 10–15 minutes from Kırkpınar, and to Kartepe there are adventure parks set in the forest. These parks generally have rope courses at different heights, a giant swing, a climbing wall and short zipline routes; some operators also offer group activities such as paintball. Because operators change often and open and close with the season, we do not name any here; you will see their signs along the road, but it is a good idea to confirm they are open before you go.

SUP on Lake Sapanca: the new favourite

Stand-up paddleboarding (SUP) has spread rapidly on Lake Sapanca over the last few years. According to a DHA news report from July 2026, an association aiming to popularise SUP on the lake organises free introductory events, and events are held at sunset where dozens of SUP boards head out onto the lake at once. The association's rule is simple: everyone who goes out on the water should wear a life jacket.

The wind on the lake should not be underestimated: in the same month, 35 people who had headed out on boats and SUPs in a sudden storm were rescued by AFAD teams. If there is a wind warning, stay on the shore, do not go far from the shore and keep your phone with you. For other lake activities, see our Lake Sapanca activities article.

How do you plan an adventure day with the family?

If you are going with children, splitting the day in two works well: the cable car and a short walk at the upper station in the cool of the morning, then rope courses suited to the children's height in the afternoon. Because some activities apply height, age and weight limits, those coming with small children can first plan just the cable car and choose activities on the spot. Other stops suited to families with children (Ormanya, the Uzunkum picnic area) are in our family holiday in Sapanca article and our Ormanya guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a zipline in Sapanca?

Yes. There is a zipline at the upper station of the Sapanca Teleferik; according to the operator's official website, ATV safari and trekking are offered in the same area. Some adventure parks on the Maşukiye–Kartepe route also have short zipline routes.

How much does the zipline in Sapanca cost?

The zipline fee is not published on the operator's official page and is separate from the cable car ticket. For the current price, we recommend calling Sapanca Teleferik on 444 6 345.

What hours are the cable car and zipline open?

According to the Sapanca Teleferik official website, the facility operates every day from 11:00 to 19:00; changes depending on weather conditions are possible.

Are the adventure activities suitable for children?

Many parks have low courses suitable for children; however, age, height and weight limits may apply on the zipline and ATV. Ask the operator before you go.

Can you do SUP on Lake Sapanca?

Yes, SUP is becoming increasingly popular on the lake. Wearing a life jacket is a must; do not head out onto the lake in windy weather, as rescue operations have taken place during sudden storms.

What should you wear for the activities?

Wear closed, non-slip shoes and clothes you can move in, and bring a spare set of clothes; for ATV and SUP, be prepared to get wet and muddy.
