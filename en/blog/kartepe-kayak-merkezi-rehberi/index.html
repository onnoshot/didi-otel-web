Kartepe Ski Resort Guide: Staying in Sapanca and Skiing in Kartepe

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

Kartepe Ski Resort is one of the closest ski centres to Istanbul and lies about 20 km, 25–30 minutes by road from Sapanca Kırkpınar. Set in the Samanlı Mountains, the resort has forest pistes of varying difficulty, chairlift and drag-lift lines, and sledding areas. Staying in Sapanca and skiing by day offers a wider choice of evenings and activities than staying on the mountain. Because season dates are set each year according to snow conditions, always check piste status before you go.

Summary
  • Distance: Kırkpınar → Kartepe Ski Resort ~20 km, 25–30 min (longer in winter conditions).
  • Facilities: Sources mention 12–17 pistes, 3 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts; sledding areas and equipment rental.
  • Season: Depends on snowfall; check opening and piste status before you go.
  • Road: Winter tyres are a must, carry chains; leave early on weekend mornings.

Where is Kartepe Ski Resort and how do you get there from Sapanca?

Kartepe Ski Resort is in Kocaeli's Kartepe district, in the Samanlı Mountains, south of Maşukiye. Coming from Sapanca, you first go to Maşukiye and then take the road that climbs the mountain. According to OpenStreetMap routing, the distance between Kırkpınar and the ski resort is about 20 km and takes 25–30 minutes in clear weather. On snowy days, especially on the winding last stretch, the time can be noticeably longer.

When does the ski season start?

The opening of the season at Kartepe depends entirely on snowfall; in some years it starts in mid-December, in others in January, and depending on snow it can last until March. Because Sapanca itself is mild (the average January low in Adapazarı is 3.5 °C), ski conditions may have formed on the mountain even if you see no snow by the lake. Before planning a weekend, check the ski resort's piste-status announcements and the weather forecast.

Kartepe for beginners and children

Some of the pistes that run through the forest are suitable for beginners. If you are skiing for the first time, we recommend taking lessons at the resort's ski schools rather than heading onto the piste alone; equipment rental is also available at the resort. For children who will not be skiing, the sledding areas are the most fun option. On busy weekends there can be waiting in the rental and lift queues, so arriving close to opening time makes for a more productive day.

Kartepe for non-skiers

You do not need to know how to ski to go to Kartepe. The sledding areas are the easiest fun for children and adults; even when there is no snow, the view from the areas near the summit of the Samanlı Mountains out towards Lake Sapanca is worth the journey. The nearby Kuzuyayla Nature Park is a lovely stop for short walks in the snowy forest in winter and a cool picnic in summer. If your group has both skiers and non-skiers, a practical arrangement is to drop the skiers off in the morning, head down with the others to the stream in Maşukiye and meet up again in the afternoon.

Weekday or weekend?

The biggest difference at ski resorts is the crowd. On weekends, and especially during the mid-year school holiday and New Year week, lift queues, equipment rental and parking get busy. If you can set aside a weekday, you will use the pistes far more comfortably. If you are going at the weekend, leaving Sapanca at around 08:30 and arriving close to opening time also lets you catch the best snow conditions of the day; in the afternoon the snow softens and the pistes start to break up. On the way back, keep the sunset time in mind (around 17:30–18:00 in December–January) so you are not caught by darkness on the mountain road.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kartepe Ski Resort from Sapanca?

It is about 20 km by road from Sapanca Kırkpınar; it takes 25–30 minutes in clear weather and may take longer on snowy days.

How many pistes does Kartepe have?

Sources give different figures: according to a 2021 AA report there are 17 pistes of 450–3,200 m, while another report says 12 pistes. The lift system consists of 3 chairlifts and 2 drag lifts.

When does the ski season start at Kartepe?

The season date depends on snowfall; it opens in December in some years and in January in others. Check the ski resort's piste-status announcements before you go.

Can I rent equipment at Kartepe?

Yes, the resort is reported to offer ski, snowboard and clothing rental as well as locker service.

Do I need chains when going to Kartepe?

On snowy and icy days winter tyres are a must on the mountain road; we recommend carrying chains.

Why stay in Sapanca for skiing?

Kartepe is 25–30 minutes from Kırkpınar; Sapanca offers a wider choice of evenings, alternatives for non-skiers such as the cable car, Ormanya and thermal springs, and a train connection.
