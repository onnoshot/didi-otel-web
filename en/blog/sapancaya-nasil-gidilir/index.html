How to Get to Sapanca: A Guide by Car, Train, Coach and Airport

3 July 2026Updated: 16 November 20265 min readSapanca

The most practical way to reach Sapanca is by private car on the TEM (Anatolian Motorway): it is about 125–135 km from the Anatolian side of Istanbul and takes 1.5–2 hours depending on traffic. For those without a car there are two train options: the Ada Ekspresi departing from Gebze stops right at Sapanca station, while the YHT (high-speed train) does not stop at Sapanca and gets off at Arifiye. Sabiha Gökçen Airport is about 95–110 km away, and Ankara about 320 km. Below you will find the journey time, pros and cons of each method, and how to cover the last kilometres to Kırkpınar.

Summary
  • By car: Sapanca toll gates on the TEM; DİDİ Otel is about 500 m from the gates.
  • By train: Marmaray + Ada Ekspresi (directly to Sapanca) or the YHT to Arifiye + taxi. Detailed train guide
  • From the airport: Sabiha Gökçen is the closest airport. Airport guide
  • From Ankara: ~2 h 36 min by YHT to Arifiye, ~3.5 hours by car. Ankara guide

How do you drive to Sapanca from Istanbul?

The most common route is the TEM (Anatolian Motorway). Head from Istanbul towards Ankara, pass İzmit, and leave the motorway at the Sapanca exit. DİDİ Otel Sapanca is in Kırkpınar, about 500 metres from the motorway's Sapanca toll gates and about 3 km from central Sapanca; in other words, you reach the hotel a few minutes after leaving the motorway.

According to map routing, it is about 125 km between Kadıköy and Kırkpınar. On a traffic-free morning this can drop to as little as 1 hour 20 minutes, but on Friday evenings and the eve of holidays, congestion between Gebze and İzmit can stretch it to 2.5 hours. If you are coming from the European side, add 15–30 minutes depending on your choice of bridge. A practical tip: setting off on Friday before 15:00 or after 20:00 often saves half an hour.

Tips on the road

  • The HGS/OGS toll system is used on the motorway; make sure your vehicle has a tag.
  • In winter the roads in Sapanca are usually open; if you are heading up to Kartepe, carry winter tyres and chains.
  • The hotel has free parking for guests.

Can you get to Sapanca by train?

Yes, but it matters which train you choose. The YHT does not stop at Sapanca; the only train that calls at Sapanca station is the Ada Ekspresi regional train running between Gebze and Adapazarı. If you take Marmaray from Istanbul to Gebze and change onto this train, Gebze–Sapanca takes about 1 hour 5 minutes. A faster alternative is to take the YHT to Arifiye and then a taxi of about 13 km to Kırkpınar. For current timetables and tips, see our guide to getting to Sapanca by train from Istanbul.

How do you get to Sapanca by coach?

There are regular coach services from Istanbul's coach stations towards Sakarya and Sapanca. Some companies drop you at their own office in Sapanca, others at stops on the motorway, so when buying a ticket be sure to ask "Where do we get off in Sapanca?" From your drop-off point you can reach Kırkpınar quickly by taxi. Because departure times and fares vary by company, we recommend getting current information from ticketing platforms or the company itself.

Getting to Sapanca from the airport

The closest airport to Sapanca is Sabiha Gökçen (SAW), a journey of about 95–110 km depending on the route. Istanbul Airport (IST) is about 170 km away by road. For a step-by-step look at public transport, train and transfer options from the airport, see our guide to getting from the airport to Sapanca.

To Sapanca from Ankara and Anatolia

For those coming from Ankara, the fastest way is to take the YHT to Arifiye (about 2 hours 36–39 minutes); from Arifiye it is about 13 km by road to Kırkpınar. By car, Ankara–Sapanca is about 320 km. Details are in our article on how to get to Sapanca from Ankara.

Can you explore Sapanca without a car?

Partly. If you stay in Kırkpınar, the lake shore and the cable car are a short taxi ride away; Maşukiye and Ormanya are also about 15 minutes by taxi. For more distant stops such as Kartepe, Poyrazlar Lake and Kuzuluk, it makes more sense to rent a car or arrange a full-day transfer. You can find how far each stop is in the table in our guide to the places to visit in Sapanca.
