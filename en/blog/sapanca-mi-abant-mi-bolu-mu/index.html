Sapanca, Abant or Bolu? A Weekend Getaway Comparison

26 August 2026Updated: 16 November 20265 min readSapanca

The short answer to Sapanca, Abant or Bolu: the closest to Istanbul is Sapanca, about 125–135 km and 1.5–2 hours from the Anatolian side. Lake Abant is about 258 km away at an altitude of 1,328 metres, and appeals to those after snow in winter and colour in autumn. Bolu's forest routes such as Gölcük and Yedigöller need a 3–4 hour drive. Sapanca makes sense for a one-night break, a train option and comfort by the lake; Abant/Bolu make sense for guaranteed snow and high-plateau air.

Summary
  • Sapanca: ~125–135 km, 1.5–2 hours; the lake is at 33 m altitude, with a mild climate; also reachable by train.
  • Abant: ~258 km, ~3–3.5 hours; a national park at 1,328 m; snowy in winter, colourful in autumn.
  • Bolu (Gölcük / Yedigöller): ~260–290 km, 3–4 hours; forest and nature park routes.

Sapanca: the closest lake getaway to Istanbul

Sapanca's greatest asset is how easy it is to reach. A few minutes after leaving the motorway you are by the lake; even without a car it is possible to come by train. This proximity makes it realistic to set off on Friday evening and spend Saturday morning at the lake. There is no single "nature spot" in the area; there are many different experiences within a small radius:

  • Lake: shoreline walks, cycling and SUP on Lake Sapanca, which covers about 45 km².
  • Cable car: a bird's-eye view of the lake on a 1,501-metre line from Kırkpınar. Cable car guide
  • Forest and waterfall: Maşukiye 10–15 minutes, Ormanya about 15 minutes.
  • Snow and skiing: Kartepe 25–30 minutes. Kartepe ski guide

As for climate, Sapanca is mild: according to the 1991–2020 normals for Adapazarı, the average July high is around 30 °C and the average January low is around 3.5 °C. This makes Sapanca a place you can visit in all four seasons, but without a guarantee of snow.

Abant: a lake on a high plateau

Lake Abant, 34 km south-west of Bolu, sits at an altitude of 1,328 metres and was declared a national park on 10 June 2022. It is known for the walking path around the lake, the trees turning yellow in autumn and its snow-covered scenery in winter. Because it is about 258 km from Istanbul, the time spent on the road increases noticeably on a one-night getaway; it is more enjoyable to think of Abant as a plan of two nights or more.

Bolu: Gölcük and Yedigöller

Gölcük Nature Park, close to Bolu centre, and Yedigöller (the Seven Lakes), further north, are favoured for long forest walks and photography. The last stretch of the road to Yedigöller is a forest track; in winter conditions access can become difficult. Because of the 3–4 hour journey from Istanbul, time on the road again takes a significant share of a weekend programme.

If you are planning a single night, Sapanca is by far the most practical choice: even on the day you return there is time left for Maşukiye or the cable car. For a sample flow, see the 1-day and 2-day itinerary.

If you have three days, two options make sense: either make Sapanca your base and add Kartepe, Kuzuluk and Taraklı (3-day itinerary), or accept the road and go to Abant/Bolu.

Autumn colours are famous at Abant, but the forests around Sapanca also start to turn from late October, and at half the distance. Details are in our Sapanca autumn guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sapanca or Abant better?

If you want a short getaway and easy access, Sapanca (~1.5–2 hours) has the advantage. If snow and high-plateau air are your priority, you might choose Abant at 1,328 m altitude (~258 km).

Which nature getaway is closest to Istanbul?

Of the three, the closest to Istanbul is Sapanca: about 125–135 km and 1.5–2 hours from the Anatolian side.

How many km is Abant from Istanbul?

Abant Lake National Park is about 258 km from Istanbul; it takes about 3–3.5 hours by car without traffic.

Which is better in winter?

Abant stands out for snowy lake views. Sapanca's lake is mild, but you can ski at Kartepe, 25–30 minutes away, so you can combine a comfortable lake stay with snow.

Which of them can you reach by train?

Of the three, Sapanca is the only place directly reachable by train: the Ada Ekspresi stops at Sapanca station, while the YHT gets off at nearby Arifiye.

Which is suitable for couples?

For short romantic getaways, Sapanca's lakeshore, cable car and boutique hotels are practical; for a longer, quieter plan, Abant is also a lovely choice.
