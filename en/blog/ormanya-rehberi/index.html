Ormanya Guide: Hobbit Houses and a Nature Park, 15 Minutes from Sapanca

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

Ormanya is a nature park set in a large forest area in the Uzuntarla neighbourhood of Kocaeli's Kartepe district. It is about 13 km by road from Sapanca Kırkpınar, 15 minutes without traffic. With its Hobbit houses, animal-watching areas and kilometres of walking and cycling trails, it is an ideal half-day stop, especially for families with children. According to information in the press, entry is free; opening hours change with the season, and according to some sources the park does not admit visitors on Mondays for maintenance. We recommend confirming the current situation before you go.

Summary
  • Location: Uzuntarla, Kartepe / Kocaeli — ~13 km, ~15 min from Kırkpınar.
  • Entry: Free according to press reports; parking and camping areas may be charged.
  • Hours: According to sources, roughly 09:00–20:00 in summer and 09:00–17:00 in winter; may be closed on Mondays.
  • Best for: Families with children, walkers, photography fans.

Where is Ormanya and how do you get there from Sapanca?

Ormanya is in the Uzuntarla neighbourhood of the Kartepe district, to the north-west of Lake Sapanca. Coming from Sapanca, you turn off towards Kartepe from the Anatolian Motorway (TEM) or the D-100; signs lead you to the park. According to OpenStreetMap routing, the road from Kırkpınar to Ormanya is about 13 km and takes 15 minutes without traffic. On weekends and holidays there can be a queue of cars at the park entrance, so going early in the morning is the most comfortable option.

According to guides in the press, the main sections of the park are as follows:

The size of the park is given differently in different sources: some reports say about 2,000 decares, others 4,000 decares. Whichever it is, it is hard to see all of it in a day; with a half-day plan you can comfortably see the Hobbit houses, the animal areas and a short walking trail.

Is there an entry fee at Ormanya?

According to news reports from 2025, entry to Ormanya is free. It is reported that a parking fee may be charged at certain times, while services such as electricity and water at the camping and caravan areas are paid. Because fee policies at municipal facilities can change, we recommend checking the latest announcements from Kocaeli Metropolitan Municipality before you go.

What time does Ormanya open, and which day is it closed?

The hours given in sources can be summarised by season as follows: roughly 09:00–20:00 in the summer months and roughly 09:00–17:00 in the winter months. Some sources write that the park does not admit visitors on Mondays for cleaning and animal care. If your plan falls on a Monday, be sure to confirm in advance; for weekend plans this is not an issue.

Hours and closing-day information are compiled from guides in the press and are not based on an official timetable. For definitive information, contact the park's operator, Kocaeli Metropolitan Municipality.

With small children, choose flat paths where you can use a pushchair; some trails are sloped and can be muddy after rain. It is a good idea to bring water, snacks and a change of clothes.

Ormanya, Maşukiye and the cable car: a full-day route

Because Ormanya, Maşukiye and Sapanca Teleferik are very close to one another, it is possible to combine all three in a single day. Go to Ormanya around opening time in the morning and see the Hobbit houses and animal areas before the crowds start. Have lunch by the stream in Maşukiye, about 10 minutes away. In the afternoon, return to Kırkpınar and take the cable car for a bird's-eye view of the lake; the cable car is open every day from 11:00 to 19:00. You can end the day with a sunset walk by the lake. The total driving time for this route does not exceed an hour; you spend most of the day in nature, not on the road.

Photography tips at Ormanya

  • Light: In the forest at midday the contrast between shade and sun is harsh; morning and late-afternoon light is softer for the Hobbit houses.
  • Crowds: On weekend afternoons there can be queues at the most popular spots; go early for empty frames.
  • Animals: Do not use flash, so as not to frighten the animals, and shoot from outside the fences.
  • Season: Spring green and autumn colours are the park's most photogenic times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ormanya?

Ormanya is in the Uzuntarla neighbourhood of Kocaeli's Kartepe district. It is about 13 km by road from Sapanca Kırkpınar, 15 minutes without traffic.

How much is the entry fee at Ormanya?

According to news reports from 2025, entry is free. Fees may be charged for parking and for services at the camping and caravan areas; confirm the current situation with Kocaeli Metropolitan Municipality.

What time does Ormanya open?

According to sources, it is open roughly 09:00–20:00 in the summer months and roughly 09:00–17:00 in the winter months. Hours may change with the season.

Is Ormanya open on Mondays?

According to some sources, Ormanya does not admit visitors on Mondays because of cleaning and animal care. If you plan to go on a Monday, confirm in advance.

What is there at Ormanya?

Hobbit houses, observation areas with animals such as deer and llamas, a children's zoo, marked walking and cycling trails, a nature school, and picnic and camping areas.

How long does it take to see Ormanya?

2–3 hours is enough for the Hobbit houses, the animal areas and a short walk; those who want to walk all of the trails should set aside a full day.
