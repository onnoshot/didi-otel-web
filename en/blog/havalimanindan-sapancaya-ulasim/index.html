Getting from the Airport to Sapanca: Sabiha Gökçen and Istanbul Airport

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

The airport closest to Sapanca is Sabiha Gökçen (SAW): depending on the route it is about 95–110 km, or between 1 hour 15 minutes and 1.5 hours by road without traffic. Istanbul Airport (IST) is about 170 km away and takes around 2 hours without traffic. With luggage, the most comfortable option is a pre-arranged transfer or a rental car; for budget travellers it is also possible to go from Sabiha Gökçen to Pendik, take the YHT high-speed train to Arifiye, and then a taxi to Kırkpınar.

Summary
  • SAW → Sapanca: ~95–110 km; 1 h 15 min – 1.5 hours by transfer or car.
  • IST → Sapanca: ~170 km; ~2 hours by transfer or car (can be much longer depending on traffic).
  • Public transport (SAW): bus to Pendik YHT station → YHT to Arifiye (~1 h 15 min) → taxi ~15 min.
  • Public transport options thin out for night arrivals; arrange your transfer in advance.

How do you get from Sabiha Gökçen to Sapanca?

1. Transfer or taxi

If you land with luggage and want to go straight to the hotel, the most comfortable option is a pre-arranged transfer. There are taxis at the airport too, but since this is an intercity journey, agree on the fare before you get in. For larger families and groups, transfers in bigger vehicles bring the cost per person down to a reasonable level. If you need help with transfer options, write to us with your flight details and we will point you in the right direction.

2. Car rental

Many car rental companies have offices at Sabiha Gökçen. Since stops such as Kartepe, Maşukiye, Ormanya and Kuzuluk are 15–45 minutes apart from one another around Sapanca, renting a car gives you great flexibility throughout your holiday. After you leave the motorway at the Sapanca exit, the hotel is about 500 metres from the toll gates; free parking is available for our guests.

3. Public transport: the YHT via Pendik

For budget-minded travellers with light luggage, you can take a bus from Sabiha Gökçen to Pendik YHT station (via İETT's airport–Pendik lines) and go on from there by YHT to Arifiye. According to the October 2026 timetable compilation, the YHT journey from Pendik to Arifiye takes about 1 hour 13–18 minutes. From Arifiye station it is then about 13 km, or 15 minutes, by taxi to Kırkpınar. Check the bus line numbers and times against İETT's current information before you travel.

4. Intercity bus

Some Sakarya-based bus companies run services between Sabiha Gökçen and Adapazarı that pass through Sapanca. Because departure times and drop-off points vary by company, confirm with the company before buying a ticket.

How do you get from Istanbul Airport to Sapanca?

Istanbul Airport is noticeably farther from Sapanca than Sabiha Gökçen (~170 km), and the route passes through Istanbul's heavy traffic and the Bosphorus crossing. If you are landing at IST, a pre-arranged transfer or a rental car is therefore the most practical option. It is possible to get into the city by public transport and reach Sapanca with a combination of Marmaray, YHT or bus, but that means several changes with luggage and takes a long time. For flights landing in the evening, allow for the journey to run well beyond its normal duration.

Tips for your airport journey

  • Plan around your flight time: Public transport options thin out for flights landing after midnight.
  • Let us know your arrival: Our check-in time is 14:00; write to us in advance about early or late arrivals and we will prepare your room accordingly (early check-in depends on availability).
  • Leave a margin on the way back: When returning from Sapanca to Sabiha Gökçen, especially on Sunday evenings, allow at least 1 extra hour for traffic.
  • Child seat: Tell us in advance if you need a child seat in your transfer or rental car.

Practical notes for guests coming from abroad

  • Language: Besides Turkish, our website is also available in English, Arabic, German and Russian, so you can read the room details in your own language.
  • Rental car and motorway: Turkey's motorways use electronic tolls via HGS/OGS. When you pick up your rental car, ask whether it has a toll tag and how the charges will be passed on to you.
  • Contact: The fastest way to reach us before you travel is WhatsApp; our number is +90 533 135 08 88.
  • Arrival time: If you arrive early after a long flight, write to us in advance for early check-in; we will help depending on availability. Otherwise you can leave your bags and stroll along the lake shore.
  • Cash and card: Some places, such as the cable car ticket office, sell tickets only on site; having a card and a little cash with you makes things easier.

When should you leave Sapanca for your return flight?

As a general rule, for a domestic flight from Sabiha Gökçen, leave Sapanca at least 3 hours before departure: that covers the roughly 1.5-hour drive, possible traffic and airport procedures. For international flights it is safer to extend this to 4 hours, and for departures from Istanbul Airport to 4.5–5 hours. On Sunday evenings and at the end of religious holidays, traffic builds up between Gebze and Pendik, so even these margins can be tight. If you are returning by public transport, plan the connection in advance: YHT from Arifiye to Pendik, then a bus on to the airport.

Where to stay if you are arriving from the airport

For those who want to rest as soon as they leave the motorway after a long flight, DİDİ Otel Sapanca is about 500 metres from the Sapanca toll gates. Breakfast is included with your stay (served as a plated breakfast at Mare Gastro; the traditional Turkish spread breakfast, serpme, is available for an extra charge), Mare Gastro in the garden is right next to you for dinner, and in pool season you can relax in Sapanca's only chlorine-free pool. The four-person Family Room stands out for larger families and the King Suit for couples. For all transport options, see our guide on how to get to Sapanca.
