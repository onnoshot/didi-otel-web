What to Do in Sapanca When It Rains: 8 Indoor and Covered Ideas

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

On a rainy day in Sapanca, the best options are the thermal pools at Kuzuluk, 35–45 minutes away; a short walk to the Maşukiye waterfalls, which roar after rain; the historic mansions of Taraklı; an ıslama köfte (soaked Turkish meatball) tour in Sapanca and Adapazarı; and a long breakfast at the hotel followed by a rest in your room. According to Adapazarı's climate normals, about half the days in December and January are rainy; but a rainy day does not always mean rain from morning to night. That is why a good plan B is enough to save a Sapanca weekend.

Summary
  • Indoors: Kuzuluk thermal pools, a long breakfast, a room with a jacuzzi, a food tour.
  • Partly covered: Taraklı's mansions and bazaar, tea in the covered cabanas.
  • When the rain stops: Maşukiye waterfalls, misty lake-shore photos.
  • Avoid: The cable car and lake activities in windy weather; muddy forest trails.

Which months are rainier in Sapanca?

Knowing the chance of rain lets you build the right plan from the start. According to the 1991–2020 Adapazarı normals from the Turkish State Meteorological Service, the average numbers of rainy days are as follows:

8 things to do in Sapanca when it rains

1. Thermal pools in Kuzuluk

Hot water is the best rescuer of a rainy day. The Kuzuluk hot springs, in Akyazı, are about 47 km (35–45 minutes) from Kırkpınar; academic measurements put the spring's surface temperature at about 51 °C. Ask in advance about the terms at the facilities offering day-use pools and private cabins.

2. An unhurried breakfast

Rain is the best excuse for an unhurried breakfast. For our guests, breakfast is included with the stay (served as a plated breakfast at Mare Gastro; the traditional Turkish spread breakfast, serpme, is available for an extra charge); ordering a second tea while watching the rain fall beyond the glass is one of Sapanca's winter rituals.

3. Resting in a room with a jacuzzi

Spending a wet afternoon in your room is not a loss but a plan. For couples, take a look at the post where we describe our rooms with a jacuzzi.

4. An ıslama köfte and kabak tatlısı tour

In Sapanca's town centre, or in Adapazarı, 20 minutes away, sit down at a warm restaurant for the geographically indicated Adapazarı ıslama köfte (a soft, sauce-soaked meatball on a bun) and kabak tatlısı (candied pumpkin dessert). Details are in our post on what to eat in Sapanca.

5. The historic mansions of Taraklı

Known for its wooden Ottoman houses and historic bazaar, Taraklı is a partly covered route you can explore even in the rain; it is easy to wander the narrow streets and stop somewhere warm. For the route and distance, see our Taraklı and Geyve day trip guide.

6. Maşukiye waterfalls when the rain stops

One nice thing about rainy days is that the waterfalls and streams in Maşukiye grow loud and full. When the rain eases, go down to the stream on a short, flat walk; wet stones and paths will be slippery, so wear shoes with a good grip. Afterwards it is a perfect time for a hot lunch at the stream-side restaurants.

7. Photos on the misty lake shore

The mist that forms over the lake after rain and the light filtering through the clouds give some of Sapanca's most atmospheric shots. The Kırkpınar shore is a few minutes from the hotel. You can find which spot to visit at what time in our photo spots guide.

8. Tea and a book in a covered corner of the garden

In light rain, the covered cabanas in the garden and the lobby are quiet corners for reading or simply listening to the rain. In the evening, Mare Gastro in the garden looks after you without your having to go out.

A rainy day with children

On a rainy day with children you need a plan to burn off their energy. When the rain eases, even a short loop along Ormanya's flat, firm-surfaced paths, a game of hide-and-seek under the Hobbit houses or counting stones by the stream in Maşukiye does them good; spare clothes and boots are a must against the mud. Some of the thermal facilities at Kuzuluk may have family pools and cabins; ask in advance about their terms for children. A note for dinner: children are not admitted to Mare Gastro in the hotel garden after 15:00, so plan evening meals with children accordingly.

The best hours after the rain

Sapanca's most striking views often come right after the rain. When the clouds clear, low banks of mist form over the lake, steam rises from the slopes of the Samanlı Mountains and the washed air extends visibility. Going down to the Kırkpınar shore as soon as it clears, or taking the cable car if the wind is calm, lets you catch the best shots of the day. In November and December it gets dark around 17:30–17:40; don't lose track of that while waiting for the rain to stop.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do in Sapanca in the rain?

The best options are the thermal pools at Kuzuluk, an unhurried breakfast, resting in a room with a jacuzzi, an ıslama köfte and kabak tatlısı tour, Taraklı's historic mansions and, once the rain stops, the Maşukiye waterfalls.

What is the rainiest month in Sapanca?

According to the 1991–2020 Adapazarı normals, the rainiest month is December, with an average of 109 mm and about 16 rainy days; July and August are the driest months.

Can you ride the cable car in the rain?

It may run in light rain, but in strong wind services can be halted for safety and cloud can hide the view. Call 444 6 345 before you go.

How far is Kuzuluk from Sapanca?

It is about 47 km by road from Kırkpınar, 35–45 minutes away.

Is it worth going to Sapanca in rainy weather?

Yes. Rainy days do not always stay rainy all day; a good plan B can be built around the thermal pools, a food tour and a rest at the hotel. The misty lake view is a beauty of its own.
