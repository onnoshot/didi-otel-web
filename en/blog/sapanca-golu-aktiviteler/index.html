What to Do at Lake Sapanca: SUP, Cycling, Walking and Birdwatching

8 July 2026Updated: 16 November 20265 min readSapanca

The most popular activities at Lake Sapanca are shoreline walks, cycling in and around Uzunkum, SUP (stand-up paddleboarding), which has spread quickly in recent years, birdwatching and watching the sunset. The lake is about 16 km long from east to west, has a surface area of 45 km² and is Sakarya's source of drinking water; so safety and respect for the lake matter in every activity on the water. Below you will find where each activity is done, the best time for it and what to watch out for.

Summary
  • On the shore: walking, cycling, picnics, sunsets — Kırkpınar, the central shore, Uzunkum.
  • On the water: SUP and paddle boats; a life jacket is a must, and do not go out on the lake in wind.
  • In nature: birdwatching (especially early in the morning), a bird's-eye view by cable car.

How big is Lake Sapanca?

Knowing the scale helps before you plan an activity. According to the data compiled on Wikipedia, the lake has a surface area of about 45 km², stretches 16 km from east to west and about 5 km from north to south; its average depth is 36 m and its deepest point is around 52 m. Cycling routes that go around the lake by road come to 45–50 km depending on the road chosen; so a "lap of the lake" is a serious half-day ride, not a short outing. For questions about the lake's depth, water level and swimming, see our guide Information about Lake Sapanca.

Can you do SUP on the lake?

Yes. SUP (stand-up paddle) has been the rising activity on Lake Sapanca over the last few years. According to a DHA news report from July 2026, an association aiming to popularise SUP on the lake is organising free introductory events, and hundreds of people are waiting in line for training. The association's basic rule is clear: everyone who goes out on the water must wear a life jacket.

Do not underestimate the wind on the lake. On the evening of 22 July 2026, 35 people who had gone out on the lake on SUPs and boats were caught in a sudden storm and rescued by AFAD teams in an operation lasting about 2.5 hours; in July 2025, 17 people were also rescued because of strong wind. The officials' warning: check the weather and wind before going out on the lake, wear a life jacket, do not overload the boat and carry a means of communication.

The best routes for cycling and walking

The most regular track on the lake shore is the municipality's cycling and walking path in Uzunkum; together with its picnic areas it is a favourite with families. Sakarya Metropolitan Municipality is carrying out work to expand the network of cycling paths around the lake; you can follow the current situation in the municipality's announcements. Short shore tours starting from Kırkpınar are ideal for an hour's walk after breakfast. Route details are in our article on cycling and walking trails.

Birdwatching at Lake Sapanca

The lake is an important habitat for waterbirds. Among the species seen on the lake, Wikipedia lists the globally protected Ferruginous duck (pasbaş patka, "near threatened") and White-headed duck (dikkuyruk, "endangered"). For someone waiting with binoculars in the first hours of the morning on the quiet, reedy shores, the lake shows a completely different face. For birdwatching enthusiasts, Acarlar Longozu (Acarlar floodplain forest) is also a lovely half-day addition.

Respecting the lake: what to watch out for at a drinking-water source?

Lake Sapanca supplies most of Sakarya's drinking water and also provides water to Kocaeli. In recent years the water level has fallen because of drought; SASKİ (the water and sewerage authority) is carrying out work to remove illegal piers and structures on the shore, shoreline clean-ups and protection work together with a scientific board. What we can do as visitors is simple:

  • Do not leave litter on the shore; use only designated areas for barbecues and fires.
  • Do not do anything that puts soap, shampoo or chemicals into the lake.
  • Do not approach reed beds and bird nesting areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do at Lake Sapanca?

The most popular activities are shoreline walks, cycling in and around Uzunkum, SUP, birdwatching, sunset photography and taking in a bird's-eye view of the lake on the cable car from Kırkpınar.

How long is the shoreline of Lake Sapanca?

The lake is about 16 km long from east to west and has a surface area of 45 km². Cycling routes that go around it by road come to 45–50 km depending on the road chosen.

Can you do SUP on Lake Sapanca?

Yes, SUP is becoming increasingly common on the lake. Wearing a life jacket is a must and you should not go out on the lake in windy weather; rescue operations were carried out in sudden winds in the summers of 2025 and 2026.

Where can you cycle at Lake Sapanca?

The most regular track is the municipal cycling and walking path in Uzunkum; short rides are also possible in Kırkpınar and on the central shore.

Which birds can be seen at Lake Sapanca?

The lake is an important habitat for waterbirds; the protected Ferruginous duck and White-headed duck are among the species recorded on the lake.

What is the best season for lake activities?

Spring and autumn are the best for cycling and birdwatching, and windless summer mornings for SUP and sunsets.
