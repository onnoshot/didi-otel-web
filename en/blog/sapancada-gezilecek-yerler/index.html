Things to Do in Sapanca: A Guide to the Lake, Cable Car, Maşukiye and Kartepe

1 July 2026Updated: 16 November 20267 min readSapanca

The top things to do in Sapanca are the shore of Lake Sapanca, the Sapanca Teleferik (cable car) departing from Kırkpınar, Maşukiye with its waterfalls, Ormanya known for its Hobbit houses, and Kartepe with its ski resort. Most of these stops are a 15–30 minute drive from Kırkpınar on the south shore of the lake, so with a journey of about 1.5–2 hours from Istanbul, Sapanca fits comfortably into a day trip or a two-night getaway.

We prepared the list below as a hotel that lives in Kırkpınar and gives guests directions every day: for each stop we wrote what to do there, how far it is and in which season it is most enjoyable.

Summary
  • Close and easy: the lakeshore, Sapanca Teleferik (lower station in Kırkpınar), Uzunkum.
  • 15–20 minutes: Maşukiye waterfalls and trout restaurants, Ormanya.
  • 25–35 minutes: Kartepe Ski Resort and Kuzuyayla, Lake Poyrazlar.
  • Half a day or more: Kuzuluk hot springs, Taraklı, Acarlar Floodplain Forest.

What can you do on the shore of Lake Sapanca?

The heart of the region is Lake Sapanca: with a surface area of about 45 km², a length of 16 km from east to west and a depth of up to 52 metres, it is one of the Marmara region's important freshwater lakes, and a large part of Sakarya's drinking water comes from here. Walking, cycling, watching the sunset and, increasingly in recent years, SUP (stand-up paddleboarding) are the favourite activities along the shore. We have collected what you can do on the lake and the safety rules in our Lake Sapanca activities article, and the frequently asked questions about depth and swimming in our information about Lake Sapanca guide.

One of the best-kept areas on the shore for picnics and cycling is Uzunkum. Those who want to catch the best light for photography can take a look at our guide to sunset spots.

Sapanca Teleferik: a bird's-eye view of the lake

The Sapanca Teleferik (cable car) is a 1,501-metre line rising from the lower station in Kırkpınar to the upper station on the Mahmudiye side; the difference in height between the two stations is 319 metres. Watching the lake and Kırkpınar from above from the cabins is the classic Sapanca experience; the upper station has a zipline, ATV tours, a walking trail and a café-restaurant. According to the operator's official website, the cable car runs every day from 11:00 to 19:00. For tickets and rules, see our Sapanca Teleferik guide. Because the lower station is in the same neighbourhood as our hotel, it is very easy to start the day with the cable car.

Maşukiye: waterfalls and the trout valley

Maşukiye, to the west of Kırkpınar on the Kocaeli border, is famous for the streams coming down from the Samanlı Mountains, its small waterfalls and its trout restaurants by the stream. The waterfalls are at their fullest in spring as the snow melts; in summer, the coolness beside the stream stands out. For routes and suggestions, see our Maşukiye and Kartepe travel guide.

Ormanya: Hobbit houses and a wildlife park

Ormanya, in the Uzuntarla neighbourhood of Kartepe, is a wildlife park set in a large forest area about 13 km from Kırkpınar. With its Hobbit houses, animal-watching areas and walking and cycling trails, it is a great half-day stop, especially for families with children. For entry, hours and closing days, read our Ormanya guide.

Kartepe and Kuzuyayla: a mountain for all four seasons

The Kartepe Ski Resort in the Samanlı Mountains is about 25–30 minutes from Kırkpınar. In winter, skiing and sledding stand out; in summer, the cool plateau air and walks in Kuzuyayla Nature Park. If you are planning to ski, our Kartepe ski guide answers questions about slopes, equipment and accommodation.

Lake Poyrazlar and Mahmudiye: away from the crowds

To the north of Adapazarı, the small Lake Poyrazlar in the forest is a stop for quiet walks and birdwatching; it is about 35 km by road from Kırkpınar. The Mahmudiye side, where the cable car's upper station is located, is known for its village character and local markets. Details are in our article on Lake Poyrazlar and hidden corners.

Which route for whom? Nature lovers, couples and families

A morning walk on the lakeshore, waterfalls and trout in Maşukiye at noon, and in the afternoon a cable car ride up to the Mahmudiye side. On the second day, a forest walk in Kuzuyayla or at Lake Poyrazlar. Relaxing at the end of the day in the chlorine-free pool in season feels great.

Lake views from the cable car, a walk on the shore at sunset and a dinner in the garden at Mare Gastro in the evening. For ideas, see our romantic weekend guide.

Hobbit houses and animals at Ormanya, a picnic at Uzunkum, trout in Maşukiye. The four-person Family Room and the three-person Triple Room are practical for larger groups; more suggestions are in our family holiday in Sapanca article.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the one place you must see in Sapanca?

The shore of Lake Sapanca is the symbol of the district. Alongside it, the Sapanca Teleferik departing from Kırkpınar, the Maşukiye waterfalls 10–15 minutes away, Ormanya about 13 km away and Kartepe 25–30 minutes away stand out.

Is it possible to see Sapanca in one day?

The lakeshore, the cable car and Maşukiye can comfortably be seen in a day. For those who also want to add Ormanya, Kartepe or Lake Poyrazlar, at least one night's stay is recommended.

Where is Sapanca Teleferik and when is it open?

The lower station is in Kırkpınar (Şehit Cevdet Koç Caddesi No:78). According to the operator's official website, the cable car runs every day from 11:00 to 19:00.

How far is Maşukiye from Sapanca?

Maşukiye is reached from Kırkpınar in about 10–15 minutes by road; the time can be longer on busy weekends.

Is Ormanya close to Sapanca?

Yes. Ormanya is in the Uzuntarla neighbourhood of Kartepe, about 13 km and around 15 minutes by road from Kırkpınar.

What is there to do in Sapanca in winter?

In winter, skiing and sledding at Kartepe, snowy lake views, a thermal break at Kuzuluk and relaxing in a warm hotel room stand out. Details are in our guide to places to visit in winter.

Where should you stay to explore Sapanca?

Kırkpınar on the south shore of the lake is a practical hub because it is a short distance from the cable car, Maşukiye, Ormanya and Kartepe; DİDİ Otel Sapanca is also in Kırkpınar.
