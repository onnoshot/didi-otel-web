New Year in Sapanca: A Planning Guide to Welcoming 2027 by the Lake

16 November 20267 min readSapanca

The 2026–2027 calendar is very convenient for a New Year holiday in Sapanca: 31 December 2026 is a Thursday and 1 January 2027 is a Friday, so a three-night long weekend from Thursday to Sunday is possible. In December the average daytime high in Sapanca is around 12 °C, with about 16 rainy days in the month; for a snowy New Year, Kartepe is 25–30 minutes away. The two most critical issues are booking early and the motorway traffic on the afternoon of 31 December. Below you will find a step-by-step plan for spending New Year in Sapanca.

Summary
  • Calendar: 31 December Thursday → 1 January Friday; 3 nights from Thursday to Sunday are possible.
  • Weather: December averages 5–12 °C with ~16 rainy days; go to Kartepe for snow.
  • Light: Sunset ~17:41 on 31 December, sunrise ~08:23 on 1 January.
  • Booking: Ask about New Year conditions (minimum nights etc.) in advance; act early.

Which day does New Year 2027 fall on?

31 December 2026 is a Thursday and 1 January 2027 is a Friday. Because Friday is a public holiday, most working people get a four-day window from Thursday evening to Sunday evening. That is an ideal scenario for Sapanca: you can set off on Thursday afternoon and spend New Year's Eve at the hotel, divide Friday and Saturday between the lake, the mountain and the thermal springs, and head home on Sunday without getting stuck in traffic.

What is the weather like in Sapanca in December and January?

Winters in Sapanca are mild; it snows by the lake from time to time, but to be sure of a snowy view you need to head into the mountains. The 1991–2020 Adapazarı normals (source: Turkish State Meteorological Service) are as follows:

In practical terms: there is a good chance of a rainy day during New Year week, but do not expect freezing cold. If you want snowy scenery, you need to head up to higher-altitude Kartepe. For ideas on rainy days, see what to do in the rain.

What can you do in Sapanca at New Year?

The first sunrise of the year: the lakeshore

On 1 January 2027 the sun rises in Sapanca at about 08:23. Getting up early after New Year's Eve may be hard, but watching the first light rise through the mist by the lake is a lovely start to the new year. The Kırkpınar shore is a few minutes from the hotel.

Snow and skiing in Kartepe

Depending on snowfall, the season at Kartepe Ski Centre usually opens in December; New Year week is the busiest time for ski resorts. Always check piste and season status before you go. Details are in our Kartepe ski guide.

Winter views from the cable car

According to its official website, Sapanca Teleferik (the cable car) is open every day from 11:00 to 19:00; ask on 444 6 345 before you go whether there are special arrangements for holidays and New Year's Day, and about wind conditions. Because the sun sets at around 17:30–17:40 in December, you can watch the sunset from the cabin.

A thermal break in Kuzuluk

One of the best ways to shake off the tiredness of New Year's Eve is the Kuzuluk thermal springs, 35–45 minutes away. It may be busy on holidays, so talk to the facility in advance.

Maşukiye and Ormanya

On a cool afternoon, a walk by the stream in Maşukiye or a visit to Ormanya with the children is a good option. Ormanya's winter hours are shorter and, according to some sources, it is closed on Mondays; since 1–3 January fall on Friday to Sunday this is not a problem, but confirm before you go.

Getting from Istanbul to Sapanca at New Year

The afternoon of 31 December is one of the heaviest traffic periods of the year leaving Istanbul. A journey that normally takes 1.5–2 hours from the Anatolian side can take much longer at these times. Three tips:

  • Leave early: If possible, set off before noon on 31 December; check-in at the hotel is at 14:00.
  • Consider the train: The Ada Ekspresi (the regional train) departing from Gebze stops directly at Sapanca station; see our guide to reaching Sapanca by train for times. Timetables may change during holiday periods, so confirm with TCDD (Turkish State Railways).
  • Take the return on Sunday morning: Return traffic builds up on Sunday afternoon.

New Year's Eve dinner

For guests who want to spend New Year's Eve without going out, Mare Gastro is in the hotel garden. You can find out whether there will be a special New Year's Eve menu or programme, and the reservation conditions, from Mare Gastro or from us when you book your stay. If you are planning a special celebration, mention it when you book and a cake, candlelight and table decoration can be arranged.

New Year with children

For families with children, the biggest advantage of spending New Year in Sapanca is the richness of the daytime programme: sledding in Kartepe, the Hobbit houses at Ormanya, the cable car and a walk by the lake. For the evening, though, note one thing: children are not admitted to Mare Gastro in the hotel garden after 15:00. You will need to plan dinner with children early or at another venue. The four-person Family Room is the most comfortable choice for families who want to spend a busy New Year's Eve in the same room.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which day does New Year 2027 fall on?

31 December 2026 is a Thursday and 1 January 2027 is a Friday. That gives a four-day window from Thursday evening to Sunday evening.

Does it snow in Sapanca at New Year?

It snows by the lake from time to time, but it is not guaranteed; the average December low is 5.3 °C. For snowy scenery you need to go up to Kartepe, 25–30 minutes away.

What can you do in Sapanca at New Year?

Highlights include the first sunrise of the year by the lake (~08:23 on 1 January), snow and skiing in Kartepe, winter views from the cable car, a thermal break in Kuzuluk, and walks in Maşukiye or Ormanya.

When should I book for New Year?

New Year is one of the busiest periods in Sapanca; we recommend booking as soon as your dates are settled and asking about seasonal conditions such as minimum nights.

Is there dinner at the hotel on New Year's Eve?

DİDİ Otel has the Mare Gastro restaurant in its garden. You should ask when booking whether there is a special New Year's Eve menu or programme.

How long does it take to get from Istanbul to Sapanca on 31 December?

A journey that normally takes 1.5–2 hours can be noticeably longer in the afternoon of 31 December because of traffic. We recommend leaving before noon or coming by train on the Ada Ekspresi.
