Meetings and Company Retreats in Sapanca: A Guide to Team Getaways Near Istanbul

16 November 20266 min readSapanca

With its 1.5–2 hour distance from Istanbul, its rail connections and its lake-and-forest setting, Sapanca is one of the most practical places for one- or two-day company meetings and team retreats. Spending the morning in a productive session in the meeting room and the afternoon on the cable car, a lake-shore walk or lunch in Maşukiye gives the team both work and room to breathe. DİDİ Otel Sapanca has a daylight-filled meeting room between the lake and the forest; if you write to us with your capacity, seating layout and technical needs, we will work out a suitable plan together.

Summary
  • Why Sapanca: 1.5–2 hours from Istanbul; you can arrive without a car by Ada Ekspresi or by YHT plus Arifiye.
  • Venue: A daylight-filled meeting room, free Wi-Fi and parking, and Mare Gastro in the garden.
  • Activities: The cable car (write to the operator for group prices), a lake-shore walk, Maşukiye, SUP.
  • Planning: Settle headcount, seating layout, equipment and meal times in advance.

Why Sapanca for a company meeting?

If the team is meeting away from the office, two things are critical: everyone should be able to get there easily, and the setting should genuinely feel "out of the city". Sapanca delivers both. The roughly 125–135 km journey from the Anatolian side means a team that sets off at 08:00 can be sitting down to its meeting at 10:00. There are options for employees travelling without a car too: the Ada Ekspresi from Gebze stops directly at Sapanca station, while the YHT high-speed train stops at Arifiye. Details are in our guide to reaching Sapanca by train.

What is the meeting room at DİDİ Otel like?

Our hotel's meeting room is a space with large windows that let in daylight, with views of the garden and the mountains. As you can see in the photos, it has wooden tables that can be arranged in a U layout, a wall-mounted screen and a seating area in one corner of the room. Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel.

How many people the room can host and in what layout, equipment needs such as a projector, sound system and flipchart, and coffee-break options all vary with the structure of the event. That is why we do not give a fixed capacity here; send us your headcount and programme on WhatsApp or at info@sapancadidiotel.com and we will come back to you with a clear quote.

Team dinner and accommodation

What brings a team together after a meeting is often dinner. Mare Gastro in the hotel garden is an option you reach without going out for team dinners; the restaurant closes at 00:00 and last orders are at 23:00. Let's discuss the details of a group menu and table layout in advance. For participants staying overnight, breakfast is included with the stay (served as a plated breakfast at Mare Gastro; the traditional Turkish spread breakfast, serpme, is available for an extra charge).

For room allocation, the Superior and Junior Suit suit single or double occupancy, and the Triple suits three people sharing. The King Suit can be considered for managers or speakers. All room types are on our rooms page.

Which season suits a meeting best?

A meeting can be held in Sapanca in any season, but the outdoor part of the programme varies with the season. Spring and autumn, thanks to mild weather and a quiet region, are the most productive periods for outdoor activities. In summer, spending the midday heat in the meeting room and the late afternoon on the lake shore is a good balance; because the area is busy at weekends, weekday dates are easier. In winter the number of rainy days rises, so the programme needs an indoor workshop and a rain plan. New Year, religious holidays and school breaks are the busiest periods in the region, so corporate events are generally scheduled outside these dates.

Hybrid attendance and digital needs

If some participants will be joining remotely, it is important to discuss the screen, camera and audio set-up in advance. Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel; if you tell us the times of sessions with video calls and the equipment required, we will plan the room layout together accordingly. Having a mobile data connection as a backup is always a good precaution for critical presentations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sapanca suitable for a company meeting?

Yes. Being 1.5–2 hours from Istanbul, with rail connections and a lake-and-forest setting, it is a practical choice for one- or two-day meetings and team retreats.

Does DİDİ Otel have a meeting room?

Yes. We have a daylight-filled meeting room with views of the garden and the mountains; capacity and seating layout are planned according to the event, so please contact us for details.

Can meeting participants come to Sapanca by train?

Yes. The Ada Ekspresi from Gebze stops at Sapanca station (Gebze–Sapanca ~1 hour 5 minutes); those coming by YHT get off at Arifiye and reach Kırkpınar by taxi in about 15 minutes.

What can a team do together?

Options such as the cable car, the zipline and ATV at the upper station, a lake-shore walk, lunch in Maşukiye, a SUP introduction and Ormanya are all within 15–30 minutes.
