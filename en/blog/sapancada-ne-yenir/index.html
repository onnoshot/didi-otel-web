What to Eat in Sapanca: Local Flavours and Where to Try Them

26 August 2026Updated: 16 November 20265 min readSapanca

The short answer to what to eat in Sapanca: the geographically indicated Adapazarı ıslama köfte (grilled meatballs served with bread soaked in meat stock), trout cooked by the stream, Abhaz cheese and walnut mezes from Circassian and Abkhaz cuisine, and for dessert the likewise registered Adapazarı pumpkin dessert (kabak tatlısı). Starting the day with a long serpme breakfast (the traditional Turkish spread) in lake air is also a fixture of the region. Below you will find what each dish is, where to look for it, and which ones hold an official geographical indication, with sources.

Summary
  • Registered flavours: Adapazarı ıslama köfte (2013), Sakarya Abhaz cheese and Adapazarı pumpkin dessert (2021).
  • Trout: At the streamside venues in Maşukiye; 10–15 minutes from Kırkpınar.
  • Breakfast: A serpme breakfast by the lake or a village-style Circassian breakfast.

What shapes Sapanca cuisine?

The table of Sapanca and its surroundings is fed by three sources: the vegetables and fruit of the fertile Sakarya plain, the fish of the lake and mountain streams, and the culinary culture of the Circassian and Abkhaz communities who settled in the region from the Caucasus in the 19th century. Traces of immigrants from the Balkans can also be seen, especially in the everyday dishes of Adapazarı's tradesmen's restaurants. So it is not at all surprising here to eat a walnut-based Circassian meze, grilled meatballs and stream trout on the same day.

Why is Adapazarı ıslama köfte special?

Islama köfte is Sakarya's best-known dish and it is officially registered: in the records of the Turkish Patent and Trademark Office, "Adapazarı Islama Köftesi" was registered as an indication of origin (mahreç işareti) on 8 January 2013 on the application of the Sakarya Chamber of Commerce and Industry. According to the registration document, its most distinctive feature is the stale bread served with the meatballs, soaked in meat stock and heated on the grill. According to accounts in the press, the dish originated from the habit of immigrants from the Balkans in the 1900s of making use of stale bread.

In the centre of Sapanca, on Mimar Sinan Caddesi in Rüstempaşa, there are restaurants serving köfte and everyday home-style dishes; one of them, Eker Lokantası, is among the places mentioned for its ıslama köfte in food guides. In central Adapazarı, ıslama köfte restaurants are far more common.

Where do you eat trout in Sapanca?

The region's classic address for trout is Maşukiye, about 10–15 minutes from Kırkpınar. At the venues set up beside the streams that flow down from the Samanlı Mountains, trout is grilled, pan-fried or cooked on a tile; it usually comes with cornbread and a seasonal salad. Since it can be busy at midday at weekends, an early lunch is a good idea.

What to eat from Circassian and Abkhaz cuisine?

Sakarya Abhaz cheese and Adapazarı pumpkin dessert received geographical indications in 2021 on the application of the Sakarya Chamber of Commerce and Industry. Circassian cuisine's Circassian chicken and mezes such as walnut beans and borlotti bean paste turn up at village-style breakfasts and in local restaurants. These mezes offer a rich choice for vegetarians.

Breakfast culture in Sapanca

Most people who come to Sapanca at the weekend start the day with a serpme breakfast that lasts for hours in lake air: village cheeses, homemade jams, honey with clotted cream, hot pastries and strong tea. The plane-tree-shaded gardens on Kırkpınar's lake shore are among the best-known places for this culture. Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sapanca's famous dish?

The region's best-known flavour is Adapazarı ıslama köfte, which received a geographical indication registration in 2013. Trout eaten by the stream in Maşukiye, Abhaz cheese, Circassian chicken and pumpkin dessert also stand out.

What is ıslama köfte?

It is an Adapazarı dish in which grilled meatballs are served with stale bread soaked in meat stock and heated on the grill; it was registered on 8 January 2013 on behalf of the Sakarya Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

Where do you eat trout in Sapanca?

The best-known place for trout is the streamside venues in Maşukiye, about 10–15 minutes from Kırkpınar.

Which Sapanca flavours have a geographical indication?

Adapazarı ıslama köfte (2013), Sakarya Abhaz cheese and Adapazarı pumpkin dessert (2021) hold geographical indication registrations.

Are there vegetarian options in Sapanca?

Yes. Circassian cuisine's walnut bean, borlotti bean and black cabbage pastes, along with rich breakfast spreads, offer plenty of choice for vegetarians.
