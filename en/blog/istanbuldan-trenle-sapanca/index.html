Getting from Istanbul to Sapanca by Train: Ada Ekspresi, High-Speed Rail (YHT) and Timetables

26 August 2026Updated: 16 November 20266 min readSapanca

Short answer: the high-speed train (YHT) does not stop at Sapanca. The only train from Istanbul that takes you to Sapanca station without changing at a second station is the Ada Ekspresi regional train departing from Gebze: take Marmaray to Gebze, board one of 6 daily services, and you reach Sapanca in about 1 hour 5 minutes. The faster but connecting option is to take the YHT to Arifiye and then a short 13 km taxi ride to Kırkpınar. Below you will find the current times and journey durations for both routes, and how to get from the station to the hotel.

Summary
  • Without a station change at Sapanca: Marmaray → Gebze, then Ada Ekspresi → Sapanca (Gebze–Sapanca ~1 h 5 min, 6 services a day).
  • Fastest train: YHT → Arifiye (~1 h 15 min from Pendik, ~1 h 45 min from Söğütlüçeşme), then a taxi of ~15 min.
  • Station to hotel: Sapanca station to Kırkpınar is about 5 km; 7–10 minutes by taxi.
  • Times can change; check the TCDD e-ticket system before buying a ticket.

Does the YHT stop at Sapanca?

No. The Ankara–Istanbul high-speed line runs close to Sapanca on its own route but does not stop there; the YHT stop in Sakarya is Arifiye. Older news items mention Sapanca as a planned YHT stop, which causes some confusion; in current operation only the regional train calls at Sapanca station. So when you say "Sapanca by train", there are two realistic routes: the regional train straight to Sapanca station, or the YHT to Arifiye and then by road to Sapanca.

How do you get to Sapanca on the Ada Ekspresi?

The Ada Ekspresi is a regional train running between Gebze and Adapazarı (Mithatpaşa), and it stops at Sapanca station. From anywhere in Istanbul you first take Marmaray to Gebze, the eastern end of the line, and change at the same station onto the Ada Ekspresi. According to the timetable published in August 2026 there are 6 services a day, and the Gebze–Sapanca leg takes about 1 hour 5 minutes on each of them.

Regional train times and fares change from season to season; before you travel, confirm them on the TCDD e-ticket system or with the TCDD call centre on 444 82 33. Note: the historic station in central Adapazarı has been temporarily closed since 3 September 2026 because of infrastructure works; trains run as far as Mithatpaşa. This does not affect passengers getting off at Sapanca.

How do you get there via the YHT and Arifiye?

If you want more frequent departures and a more comfortable journey, you can take the YHT and get off at Arifiye. The YHT stops in Istanbul are Halkalı, Bakırköy, Söğütlüçeşme, Bostancı, Pendik and Gebze. According to a compilation dated October 2026, there are 11 YHT services a day between Istanbul and Arifiye; the journey from Söğütlüçeşme to Arifiye takes about 1 hour 41–47 minutes, and from Pendik about 1 hour 13–18 minutes. The same source lists the economy ticket as starting from 380 TL; because TCDD uses dynamic pricing, it varies by day and time.

From Arifiye station to Kırkpınar it is about 13 km by road, around 15 minutes without traffic according to map routing. A taxi from in front of the station is the most practical way. If you message us your arrival time in advance, we can point you in the right direction for a taxi.

How do you get from Sapanca station to the hotel?

Sapanca station is in the town centre, very close to the lake. It is about 5 km between the station and Kırkpınar; by taxi it takes 7–10 minutes. DİDİ Otel Sapanca is in Kırkpınar, between the lake and the forest, about 3 km from central Sapanca. If you are arriving late at night (for example on the 21:55 train), we recommend arranging your taxi in advance; our check-in time is 14:00 and check-out is 12:00.

What can you do in Sapanca without a car?

Arriving by train doesn't stop you from exploring Sapanca. The easiest stops for travellers without a car are:

  • Sapanca Teleferik (cable car): Its lower station is in Kırkpınar; details are in our cable car guide.
  • Walks along the lake: You can stroll or cycle along Kırkpınar and the central shoreline. For routes, see our article on cycling and walking trails.
  • Maşukiye and Ormanya: 10–20 minutes away by taxi; our guides to Maşukiye and Ormanya will help you plan a route.

If you would like to plan the weekend hour by hour, our 1-day and 2-day itineraries can also be adapted for those arriving by train.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the YHT stop at Sapanca?

No. The high-speed train does not stop at Sapanca; the YHT stop in Sakarya is Arifiye. To reach Sapanca station without a second change of station, you need the Ada Ekspresi regional train departing from Gebze.

How do you get from Istanbul to Sapanca by train?

Take Marmaray to Gebze and change onto the Ada Ekspresi; Gebze–Sapanca takes about 1 hour 5 minutes. Alternatively, take the YHT to Arifiye and then a taxi of about 13 km to Kırkpınar.

What time does the Ada Ekspresi leave Gebze?

According to the August 2026 timetable, there are 6 services a day from Gebze, at 07:25, 11:00, 13:10, 16:35, 17:40 and 20:50. Because times can change, confirm them on the TCDD e-ticket system.

What is the last train from Sapanca to Istanbul?

According to the August 2026 timetable, the last regional train from Sapanca towards Gebze leaves at 21:23 and arrives in Gebze at 22:26.

How long does it take to get to Arifiye by YHT?

About 1 hour 13–18 minutes from Pendik and about 1 hour 41–47 minutes from Söğütlüçeşme. From Arifiye to Kırkpınar it is about 13 km by road.

How far is Sapanca station from Kırkpınar?

It is about 5 km between Sapanca station and Kırkpınar; it takes 7–10 minutes by taxi.
