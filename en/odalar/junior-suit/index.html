Room Details

The Junior Suite brings comfort and style together with a king bed and an elegant sitting corner. A resting nook with two armchairs and a small table creates the ideal spot to enjoy your coffee with a view.

With its balcony and warm-toned design, the Junior Suite is a more compact option than the King Suite while keeping the comfort of a suite. Its bathroom has a rain shower (it doesn't include the King Suite's in-room jacuzzi).
