FAQ

What are the check-in and check-out times at DİDİ Hotel Sapanca?

Check-in is at 14:00 and check-out at 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out may be available on request.

How far is DİDİ Hotel Sapanca from the town centre and the airport?

The hotel is 3 km from Sapanca centre, 500 m from the Sapanca toll gates on the İstanbul-Ankara motorway, 15 km from Sakarya centre, and about 95 km from Sabiha Gökçen Airport.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes, a generous breakfast buffet is included with every stay.
